claims and masks so that 71 narrative expert-validation rows become executable
without treating unsupported expert output as truth.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Exactly 71 stable claim records map one-to-one to the five source-reviewed
   expert-validation tables.
2. Every claim records a valid source locator, evidence class, review status,
   reviewer/version, and literal/semantic inclusion mask.
3. Incorrect, inferred, and unverifiable expert fields are excluded from
   literal truth; source-supported disagreements and safer parser refusals are
   preserved.
4. Case/page totals reconcile with P00-US04 and duplicate/missing/unclassified
   claims fail closed.
5. Deterministic reload produces identical canonical claim hashes.
