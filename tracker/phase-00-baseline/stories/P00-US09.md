## User story

As a regression owner, I want a finite control registry so that each confirmed
gap is tested against target, related-positive, non-target, and
negative/ambiguous evidence rather than a single benchmark example.

## Finite scope

- Exactly 25 primary gap/story mappings from the frozen gap-to-story matrix.
- Exactly 109 case-gap rows from the 15 reviewed case reports.
- Four required role assignments per primary gap: target, related positive,
  non-target regression, and negative or ambiguous control.

## Out of scope

Parser behavior, milestone execution, new source acquisition, and Phase 1.

## Acceptance criteria

1. All 25 gap owners have exactly one complete four-role control set: 100
   required role assignments.
2. All 109 case-gap rows resolve to a registered gap and reviewed case/claim
   locator; no row is silently dropped.
3. Role assignments distinguish expected capability behavior from non-target
   stability and unsupported/ambiguous rejection.
4. Missing roles, unknown gaps/stories/cases/claims, duplicate role ownership,
   and unsupported truth promotion fail closed.
5. Canonical reload is deterministic and every referenced claim belongs to the
   completed 210-claim corpus.
