## User story

As a benchmark reviewer, I want versioned claim and inclusion-mask contracts so
that source-supported, derived, incorrect, inferred, and unverifiable expert
content cannot be silently mixed in one acceptance denominator.

## In scope

- Versioned reviewed-claim, review-batch, source-locator, reviewer/version, and
  inclusion-mask records.
- Typed evidence and review-status vocabularies with explicit scoring effects.
- Physical/printed page identity and coordinate-convention fields.
- Backward-read projection for P00-US02 catastrophe truth.
- Positive and invalid synthetic contract fixtures.

## Out of scope

- Registering the 210 reviewed claims from the 15 case reports; population
  remains owned by P00-US06 through P00-US08.
- Control-role mapping, parser runs, or production schema changes.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Every claim has stable identity, case/page/region locator, claim type,
   evidence class, review status, reviewer/version, and literal/semantic scoring
   masks.
2. Incorrect, inferred, and unverifiable claims cannot enter literal-parity
   denominators; justified derived claims require method and tolerance.
3. Coordinate convention, physical page, and optional printed-page identity
   are explicit and validated.
4. Duplicate IDs, impossible pages/regions, unknown classes, missing reviewers,
   contradictory masks, and unsupported truth promotion fail meaningfully.
5. The P00-US02 catastrophe truth projects into the new contract without
   changing its frozen bytes or evidence semantics.
6. Contracts are deterministic, benchmark-only, and have no public API or
   production serializer effect.
