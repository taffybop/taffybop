## User story

As an operator, I want only eligible regions sent to an approved visual-model
adapter, so that resource use, data egress, cost, and end-to-end latency remain
governed and intentional.

## Business value

Concentrates optional inference where deterministic analysis has an evidenced
gap and makes local-versus-hosted selection predictable, reviewable, and
budget-aware.

## Problem statement

The local and hosted adapters expose bounded transports but do not decide which
regions qualify or which adapter policy permits. A broad model pass can process
complete regions, leak disallowed content, or exceed area, resource, timeout,
and cost limits before grounding is considered.

## In scope

- Eligibility from approved Phase 05 capability/quality signals, unresolved
  concerns, content type, confidence, and evidence gaps.
- Deterministic `skip`/`local`/`hosted` selection with reason codes.
- Pre-dispatch area, timeout, resource, cost, and data-policy safety limits.
- Exactly one bounded adapter invocation for an eligible routing decision.
- Route, skip, policy-deny, and adapter-failure accounting.

## Out of scope

- Grounding or structurally validating returned observations.
- Merging observations into IR or canonical output.
- Guaranteeing the final deterministic fallback.
- All-page inference, model training, or unapproved provider selection.

## Technical approach

Build a pure routing decision from region evidence, capability signals, adapter
availability, policy, and remaining budgets. Persist the decision and its
inputs, reserve applicable budgets before dispatch, and invoke at most one
adapter without interpreting or merging its response.

## Benchmark evidence — LlamaParse-15

- Cases/regions: `uber-earnings` page 1 no-caption photograph, page 2 flag grid
  and unstructured chart regions, and page 3 ambiguous topology diagrams.
- Current behavior: unresolved pixel semantics, chart structure, and diagram
  associations coexist with already complete explicit titles/endpoints; broad
  escalation would reprocess good source evidence and increase duplication.
- Generalized behavior: route only an evidenced unresolved region/capability
  gap, never the whole page or already supported explicit content, with stable
  reason and budget accounting.
- Acceptance/tests/metrics/performance: positive eligible visual gaps,
  non-target explicit labels/complete tables, and policy/budget negatives;
  measure escalated area, calls, routing p50/p95, memory, and cost separately;
  routing timing is diagnostic only.
- Gaps: `GAP-VISUAL-001`, `GAP-CHART-001`, `GAP-CHART-002`,
  `GAP-DIAGRAM-001`, and `GAP-DIAGNOSTICS-001`.

## Acceptance criteria

1. With routing or both adapters disabled, zero regions are sent to a model.
2. Every eligible fixture routes exactly once to the policy-selected adapter;
   ineligible, already-supported, or disallowed fixtures produce zero calls.
3. Local/hosted selection is deterministic for identical evidence, policy,
   adapter availability, and remaining budgets.
4. Area, timeout/resource, cost, and data-policy limits are checked before
   dispatch, with zero calls after any applicable budget is exhausted.
5. Every route/skip/deny/failure records a stable reason code, selected adapter
   if any, region area, and reserved/actual cost without payloads or secrets.
6. Router output remains an unmerged observation envelope for P06-US05; this
   story does not change JSON, Markdown, text, IDs, order, or source evidence.
7. Eligibility distinguishes an unresolved visual claim from adjacent complete
   explicit evidence: the approved photograph/flag/chart/diagram regions route,
   while printed labels/endpoints and complete deterministic tables do not.

The sole operative latency benchmark is
[`latency-reference-v1.md`](../../benchmarks/llamaparse-15/latency-reference-v1.md).

samples per applicable case and require candidate p50 and nearest-rank p95 each

failures, no corpus-average masking, and unchanged required quality/reliability.
Non-comparable cases are `Unmeasured/Blocked`; routing timings and old local
baselines cannot substitute. Safety timeouts and all non-latency limits remain
independently blocking.

## API and schema compatibility

Routing is internal/default-off and does not merge its envelope into public
output. Public v1 JSON, Markdown, and text remain unchanged in this story.

## Risks

- Poor eligibility thresholds can waste cost or miss useful escalation.
- Policy precedence can select the wrong adapter if rules are ambiguous.
- Concurrent budget reservations can overspend without atomic accounting.
- Adapter availability can change between selection and invocation.

## Rollback

Disable routing to stop all model calls. Local/hosted adapters may remain
installed but unused, and no public output requires router envelopes.

focused and cumulative P06-US01 through P06-US04 suites pass; campaign-scale
