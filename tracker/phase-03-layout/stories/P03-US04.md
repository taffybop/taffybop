# P03-US04 — Resolve relationship-aware reading order and bboxes

Status: Done  
Story points: 5  
Phase: Phase 3 — Layout  
Priority: High  
Dependencies: P03-US03, P01-US03  
Feature flag: `parser.layout.relationship_order.enabled`

## User story

As a sequential-content consumer, I want relationships and finite page-space
geometry reflected in reading order, so that side-aware captions and notes
remain attached to their owners exactly once.

## Business value

Produces stable RAG/Markdown order and prevents elements from claiming text
outside their bboxes.

## Problem statement

The predecessor still exposes flattened Docling order in several reviewed
multi-region pages, places the physical-p2 timetable title fragments after
their table, and appends one independently off-bbox `RESEARCHARTICLE` token to
clinical `p1-i14`.

## In scope

- Relationship-aware atomic bundles and bounded geometric order constraints.
- Bbox containment/ownership validation without bbox widening or ungrounded
  content mutation; exact source-proven outside-bbox contributions may be
  suppressed and finite disjoint nested source fragments may be reordered.
- Duplicate-anchor, ambiguity, limit, ownership, and cycle concerns.
- Exact backend/canonical/frontend order parity.

## Out of scope

- General semantic reordering without evidence.
- Multi-page table merging.

## Technical approach

Run a final presentation projector after P03-US01–US03. Build atomic
side-aware owner bundles, add only trusted typed or unambiguous
finite-geometry edges, perform stable topological ordering, rewrite page item
order plus contiguous `reading_order`, reject only exact independently
source-proven outside-bbox scalar contributions, and validate a complete
candidate IR before commit. The accepted contract is
[P03-relationship-order-policy.md](../decisions/P03-relationship-order-policy.md).

## Acceptance criteria

1. The fixed reviewed denominator passes 41/41 ordered pairs across
   catastrophe, timetable, clean-energy, purchase, component, ESG, clinical,
   manufacturing, and finance.
2. Table/above captions precede owners; below-visual captions follow owners;
   notes follow owners and any accepted below caption. All remain contiguous,
   distinct, and source-grounded.
3. No element claims evidence outside owned/linked bboxes. IDs, bboxes,
   evidence, relationships, winners, and suppression state are byte-equivalent
   by ID. Value/Markdown/nested/canonical order may change only for two fixed
   source-grounded corrections: clinical `p1-i14` excludes the off-bbox
   `RESEARCHARTICLE` suffix while retaining raw evidence, and clean-energy
   `p1-i1` orders its two existing nested records and parent presentation as
   report title then `Overview`.
4. Cycles, ambiguity, invalid geometry, ownership conflict, and limit overflow
   preserve the exact affected predecessor page with sanitized bounded
   concerns.
5. Repeated runs produce identical IDs, order, contiguous ranks, canonical
   views, and diagnostics; backend arrays, Markdown, canonical output, and
   frontend legacy/canonical paths agree.

## 2026-08-14 source-partition and preamble-order correction

The requester-authorized Clinical page-one amendment supersedes only the
Clinical clause in acceptance criterion 3 that required deletion of the
off-owner `RESEARCHARTICLE` suffix. Source inspection established that the
content is a legitimate `RESEARCH ARTICLE` label. It must be retained as a
separate source-proven item when a closed partition is established, not
deleted. The old exact-deletion assertion remains historical and is replaced
by the fail-closed contract below.

The reusable fused-text partition requires exactly two complete same-page
character spans, point-unit finite geometry, disjoint column ownership, unique
native source lines and characters, one immediate same-column heading anchor,
and no intervening independent owner. It preserves the original owner and
creates one attributable detached contributor with deterministic lineage.
Partial spans, missing or reused lineage, repeated/conflicting text, overlap,
cross-page/unit geometry, multiple anchors, or any ambiguity return the fused
predecessor without silent loss.

A separate generic order rule recognizes one broad heading-led main preamble,
one connected narrow sidebar on exactly one side, and a later main-column
heading as the body barrier. It emits only the edges needed to order main
preamble, sidebar, then body. Mirrored geometry is supported; two-sided,
disconnected, distant, multi-lead, missing-barrier, or cyclic cases retain the
predecessor order.
