# P03-US02 — Separate visual captions from internal children

Status: Done  
Story points: 5  
Phase: Phase 3 — Layout  
Priority: High  
Dependencies: P01-US02, P03-US01  
Feature flag: `PARSER_LAYOUT_VISUAL_RELATIONSHIPS_ENABLED`

## User story

As a visual-document consumer, I want external captions distinct from internal
visual text, so that titles retain order and chart noise does not become captions.

## Business value

Fixes Exhibit 8 title merging and improves chart, diagram, screenshot, and image
semantics without a new model.

## Problem statement

`_visual_item()` loops over both `captions` and `children`, producing a caption
containing the true title plus `er cas` and `C`.

## In scope

- Declared caption versus child roles.
- Geometry consistency and separate evidence/bboxes.
- `caption_of` and `contains` relationships.

## Out of scope

- Repairing child text.
- Chart classification or values.
- Generated image descriptions.

## Technical approach

Honor graph roles first and use inside/outside geometry as validation. Project
external captions separately; retain children inside the region evidence graph.
The binding policy and additive API are fixed in
[P03 visual-relationship policy](../decisions/P03-visual-relationship-policy.md).

- Eligible public owners are presented `image`, `chart`, or `diagram` elements.
- A declared `caption_of` edge may project only when finite, same-page,
  same-unit geometry is external, horizontally aligned by at least 20%, overlaps
  no more than 20% of the smaller region, and is within 72 points.
- Captions above an owner are immediately before it; captions below an owner
  are immediately after it. Ambiguous side placement remains evidence-only.
- A declared `contains` edge is never reclassified as a caption. A uniquely
  owned child with at least 80% of its area inside the visual may be exposed in
  bounded `contained_items`; boundary/outside conflicts remain retained IR
  evidence with concerns.
- When one source node is declared through both roles, an externally grounded
  caption role wins once; its redundant `contains` route is diagnostic.
- Successfully classified visual owners rebuild public `value` and `md` only
  from existing accepted `ocr_text` when `include_ocr_in_primary is True`.
  Scalar merged caption fields are removed and the owner bbox never expands.
- Processed owners carry `layout_visual_relationships_projected=true`; only
  marked owners use the new frontend suppression rules. Primary OCR additionally
  requires complete accepted same-unit diagnostic grounding inside the owner.
  Because the OCR crop and source-layout crop are independently rounded, an
  accepted owner diagnostic may cross a `pt` owner edge by at most 1.0 point;
  cross-unit, greater-than-1-point, and foreign-owner observations fail closed.
- Raw caption/child provenance is bounded and fail-closed. Generated, model,
  malformed, unknown, inherited-only, and unscannable raw nodes cannot gain
  trust from matched legacy evidence. A bbox-grounded inferred-punctuation
  fallback is permitted only for contained children, never captions.
- Public caption and `contains` relationship IDs use public endpoint IDs.
  Every endpoint resolves to a page item or that owner's `contained_items`.
- Physical captions shared across a table and visual fail closed for both
  story projections under a common preflight arbitration.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Exhibit 8 title is a separate caption element exactly once.
2. `er cas` and `C` are not caption content.
3. Internal children keep original bboxes/provenance.
4. Combined elements cannot claim a bbox excluding their text.
5. Natural-image OCR remains subordinate unless explicitly promoted.
6. Explicitly promoted OCR survives at most 1.0 point of source-crop rounding
   drift exactly once in JSON, Markdown, canonical output, and rendered UI;
   greater drift, cross-unit boxes, and another owner's OCR remain excluded.

## API and schema compatibility

Flag-on item count/order changes; flag-off remains exact. A projected caption
uses the P03-US01 top-level caption shape. Visual owners add `caption_ids`,
`contains_ids`, `contained_items`, and typed `relationships`. Nested children
retain ID, value, bbox, source, confidence, `presentation_role=subordinate`,
`contained_by`, and relationship metadata, but never render as document prose.
Canonical presentation emits the promoted caption and visual as separate
blocks in the same geometry-aware order and records the relationship ID on
both. The visual block contains only authorized visual OCR.

## Risks

- Graph labels can be wrong; geometry rules may be layout-specific.
- Projection amplification and pairwise overlap work are bounded: 64 captions
  and 256 contained children per owner, 512 visual owners and 512 caption
  candidates per page, 128 same-text caption candidates per page, 64 KiB per
  caption, and 256 KiB serialized `contained_items` per owner. Overflow rejects
  the complete affected set before pairwise work and reports sanitized counts,
  limits, IDs, and hashes only.

## Rollback

Disable `PARSER_LAYOUT_VISUAL_RELATIONSHIPS_ENABLED` and use exact legacy
flattening. The flag requires shared IR normalization and is independent from
the table-caption flag.

## 2026-08-14 compact visual-label correction

The user-authorized Clinical physical-page-1 release slice extends the
existing “explicitly promoted OCR” contract without changing its ownership
boundary. A visual may present its own label exactly once through either:

- native source lines that are completely and uniquely owned by graph-bound
  children inside the same visual; or
- compact OCR whose complete text is confirmed by bounded independent passes,
  lies inside the owner, satisfies the compact size/classification and word
  limits, and closes over the same source characters and contributors.

The public owner, contained children, evidence, canonical block, and rendered
label must agree. Generated descriptions, partial or conflicting OCR,
independent text, missing/cross-unit geometry, oversized unclassified images,
unclosed children, and pass disagreement remain subordinate or preserve the
predecessor. This is a generic owner-evidence capability; no Clinical name,
hash, page, item ID, label wording, or coordinate is a production decision.
