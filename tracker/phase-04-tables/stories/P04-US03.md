## Release-first acceptance criteria

This section is authoritative for the current release.

- Implemented state: the default-off flag, dependency validation, and pipeline
  hook perform bounded adjacent-page continuation scoring across compatible
  grids, explicit headers, relative geometry, page boundaries, and captions.
- Release behavior creates a derived table only with multiple supported
  signals, retains normative page-local evidence and provenance, removes only
  verified repeated headers from the derived view, and safely refuses
  ineligible, ambiguous, or malformed candidates.
- Dependencies/sequencing: requires completed P04-US02 and P04-US04 behavior.
- Required basic validation: one verified two-page continuation merges with
  original page/bbox provenance, one unrelated adjacent pair does not merge,
  one ambiguous pair retains page-local output with a concern, serializer
  output is coherent, and flag-off remains page-local.
- Deferred: exhaustive continuation corpora, false-merge campaigns, detailed
  output-size/RSS/latency accounting, adversarial variants, and evidence
  manifests.

The usable derived merge and reliable refusal path are exercised through the
public flow; deferred qualification is not part of this release-first
completion.

## Release-first completion comment

Completed with bounded adjacent-page scoring across source grid, explicit
header topology, relative geometry, page-boundary placement, and continuation
caption signals. Supported chains retain both page-local tables as normative
evidence and add one coherent derived grid with original cell and row page,
bbox, evidence, and source identities; repeated headers appear once in the
derived view while their local evidence remains intact. Incompatible,
ambiguous, ineligible, and malformed inputs fail locally without a false merge,
and flag-off is byte/identity preserving. Focused story, prior Phase 04,
serializer, caption, reading-order, and representative public parse checks
passed enabled and disabled. Deeper performance, security, campaign, evidence,
adversarial, and independent-review qualification remains deferred to
post-release hardening; it is not claimed as passed.

## User story

As a financial-document consumer, I want verified continued tables merged across
pages, so that rows remain usable without combining unrelated tables.

## Business value

Improves long statements and reports while protecting structure, provenance, and
page citations.

## Problem statement

Multi-page tables are a target capability, but unsafe merging can fabricate
headers, spans, or row associations.

## In scope

- Continuation evidence: headers, columns, geometry, captions, page adjacency.
- Page-preserving merged representation and explicit `continued_from`.
- Refusal/concern for ambiguous candidates.

## Out of scope

- Non-adjacent semantic table joins.
- Reconstructing omitted rows.

## Technical approach

Require multiple independent structural signals, retain original page-local
tables/evidence, and expose a derived merged view only above threshold.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Verified adjacent continuations merge with correct headers/rows.
2. Unrelated adjacent tables never merge in negative fixtures.
3. Every merged row/cell retains original page and bbox.
4. Repeated continuation headers are represented once canonically but remain evidence.
5. Flag-off output remains page-local.
6. Only a P04-US04-gated canonical table may enter continuation scoring; chart,
   form, unresolved, or structurally failed candidates cannot merge.
7. Merged HTML, Markdown, rows, CSV, and JSON preserve one semantic grid and
   page-local evidence.

## Benchmark evidence — LlamaParse-15

- Gaps: `GAP-TABLE-002`, `GAP-TABLE-003`, `GAP-ORDER-001`,
  `GAP-PROVENANCE-001`, `GAP-DIAGNOSTICS-001`, and
  `GAP-SERIALIZATION-001`.
- Source/current behavior: `ny-timetable` physical p1–p3 are homologous
  13-column/50-row schedule candidates but contain page-local structural/OCR
  defects, so they must not merge before P04-US04 gating. `finance-10k` p1–p3
  (printed 28/30/32) and `postal-10k` financial pages (printed 46/49) are
  adjacent but semantically distinct statements and are false-merge controls;
  clinical p2/p4 tables are non-adjacent controls.
- Generalized behavior: continuation scoring runs only on gated, structurally
  valid tables and requires adjacency, compatible canonical grids,
  header/caption evidence, and page-preserving provenance. Original page items
  remain normative evidence.
- Test slice: an approved reviewed/synthetic true continuation positive;
  timetable after local repair as related positive candidate; finance/postal
  non-targets; changed-column, missing-page, chart/form, and ambiguous-caption
  negatives.
- Acceptance/metrics/performance: 100% approved true merges, 0 false merges,
  100% merged-row page/bbox provenance, and full representation parity. Record
  local M0 RSS deltas against timetable 1,944.0 MiB and finance 1,802.7 MiB.
  The sole latency benchmark is the
  [`Phase 04 LlamaParse latency supersession`](../decisions/P04-LlamaParse-latency-canonical-supersession.md),
  bound to
  [`LlamaParse latency reference v1`](../../benchmarks/llamaparse-15/latency-reference-v1.md).
  Before Definition of Done, refresh every applicable case with at least five
  interleaved candidate/Llama observations and require candidate p50 and
  inclusive nearest-rank p95 to be no greater than paired Llama p50/p95 per
  case. Initial one-sample values and local M0/flag-off/stage/component timings
  are planning or diagnostics only. Do not average away or drop failures;
  semantically incomparable input/output is **Unmeasured/Blocked**, and all
  quality and reliability gates remain independently mandatory.
- Flag/rollback: `parser.tables.multi_page_merge.enabled`; disabling it removes
  the derived merged view and leaves page-local tables unchanged.
