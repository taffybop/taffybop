## User story

As a structured-data consumer, I want explicit repeated cells preserved unless
source geometry proves a span, so that row values are not silently blanked.

## Business value

Protects our source-faithful Exhibit 7 advantage and prevents incorrect lookups
caused by fabricated spans.

## Problem statement

The benchmark converts five visible `United States` cells into one row span.
Current regressions do not explicitly guard against that failure. The 15-case
review also adds dense rotated timetables, multilevel financial/clinical
headers, multiline logical rows, bottom-boundary omissions, and an irregular
form coverage grid. These expand the cell/span invariant and fixture work while
remaining bounded to already-selected table regions.

## In scope

- Span evidence invariants.
- Explicit repeated-cell preservation.
- Dense/rotated and multilevel header-cell ownership.
- Multiline visual wraps versus logical row identity.
- Isolated ruled and borderless cell/span fixtures plus addressable form-grid
  observations that remain non-canonical while topology is unresolved.
- Cell bbox/provenance and serializer assertions.

## Out of scope

- Candidate engine reconciliation.
- Multi-page tables.

## Technical approach

Require source grid/border/merged-cell evidence before producing row/column spans;
keep repeated values as independent cells otherwise.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Exhibit 7 has 30 cells and five `United States` values.
2. Finance/postal multilevel period headers and legitimate merged-cell fixtures
   retain source-supported row/column spans.
3. A visually wrapped label remains one logical row; repeated adjacent text
   never creates blank cells or fabricated spans.
4. Each timetable page retains 13 columns and 50 service rows after a canonical
   table is selected, or emits a targeted structural concern without shifted
   rows.
5. The explicitly enumerated postal and clinical row/column/header/span
   denominators are exact. Postal's final `FERS` row stays inside the table.
   ACORD's reviewed region bbox and visible header observations remain
   addressable, but its form-owned cell topology, ownership, spans, cell
   bboxes, and provenance must remain unresolved with targeted concerns and
   cannot satisfy canonical-table accuracy.
6. HTML, rows, CSV, Markdown, JSON cells, spans, and cell bboxes agree on
   exhaustive exact-cell oracle fixtures. For Exhibit 7, each of the 30 public
   five-key `pt` bboxes must exactly match the hash-bound Docling
   `source_content_bbox` truth within the existing `0.011 pt` numeric slack and
   must remain wholly contained in the unchanged Phase-00 `grid_slot_bbox`;
   neither role may substitute for the other. HTML/Markdown must retain exact
   `scope="col"` or `scope="row"` ownership. A real fixture without exhaustive
   cell truth is excluded from that numerator and denominator and must retain
   its dimension-specific unresolved concern.
7. Every span decision records source evidence or a concern.
