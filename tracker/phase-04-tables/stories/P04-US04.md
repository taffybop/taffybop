  deterministic structural and ownership decisions across canonical tables,
  charts/visuals, forms/key-value regions, and unresolved alternatives.

## User story

As a structured-data consumer, I want only source-supported table candidates
emitted as canonical tables, so that charts, forms, and arbitrary aligned labels
do not become misleading grids while real borderless tables remain recoverable.

## Business value

Reduces false structured data and improves coverage of common key-value,
financial, form, and borderless layouts.

## Problem statement

The health bubble chart is emitted both as a chart and a false one-column table.
The component operating-condition block is not emitted as a table/key-value
structure, while dense timetable tables lose an entire column and row without a
table-level concern.

## In scope

- Candidate features for grid, alignment, cell coverage, region type, and
  competing chart/form ownership.
- Canonical table, form/key-value, visual fallback, or unresolved alternatives.
- False-table concerns and geometry-aware duplicate suppression.

## Out of scope

- Chart value reconstruction.
- Form field extraction beyond the P03-US06 contract.
- Multi-page continuation.

## Technical approach

Score reconciled candidates against explicit cell/rule/alignment evidence and
typed region ownership. Require stable rows/columns and source coverage before
canonical emission; retain unresolved candidates and reasons rather than
forcing a table.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The health bubble chart emits one chart and zero canonical tables over the
   same region.
2. The component operating conditions emit a source-faithful two-column
   key-value/table alternative without fabricated headers.
3. ACORD form regions remain forms; its true coverage grid stays available as a
   table candidate.
4. All three timetable pages retain 13 columns and 50 service rows or fail
   closed with an explicit structural concern.
5. Overlapping table/chart/form candidates have traceable scores, source bboxes,
   and deterministic selection.
6. Flag-off behavior and public schema remain compatible.
