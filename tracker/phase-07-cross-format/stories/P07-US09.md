## Release-first acceptance criteria

- Current state: no future-adapter registry gate exists.
- Production work: require a compact capability/compatibility manifest at
  registration/advertisement and reject missing grounding, limits, fallback,
  serialization or rollback declarations; provide test adapters.
- Sequence: after P07-US08; closes the Phase 07 extension boundary.
- Basic validation: one conforming future adapter registers and handles a
  representative request, one adapter missing each essential declaration class

  disabling the gate preserves current registered adapters.
- Deferred: exhaustive mutant matrix, benchmark/resource gates, retained

This section supersedes stricter release validation requirements below.

## Release-first completion comment

Completed the default-off future-adapter compatibility gate on top of the
versioned Phase 07 registry. Candidate adapters must provide an immutable,
compact declaration covering grounding/provenance, resource limits,
non-execution/network policy, native-first fallback/reconciliation,
serialization compatibility, and rollback ownership before registration or
advertisement. A conforming test adapter handles a representative dispatch;
targeted mutants missing each essential declaration class fail closed with
stable reason codes, stale/unregistered formats are not advertised, and
disabling the future gate preserves the approved built-in/current adapter
allowlist without admitting candidates. The focused P07-US09 run passed 8 tests
and the cumulative P07-US01–US09 run passed 103 tests, both with 0 failures, 0
skips, and 1 existing Starlette `httpx` deprecation warning. Deferred exhaustive
mutant, benchmark/resource, retained-evidence, campaign, and independent-review
work was not run and is not claimed as passed.

## User story

As a parser maintainer, I want every future adapter blocked behind reusable
conformance and compatibility gates, so that adding a format cannot bypass
grounding, security, fallback, or serialization guarantees.

## Business value

Turns the Phase 07 contracts into an enforceable extension policy and prevents
incomplete adapters from being advertised, configured, or released.

## Problem statement

A reusable harness exists, but without registration and release gates a future
adapter could still omit transforms/provenance, duplicate fallback content,
fabricate unsupported structure, exceed resources, or expose incompatible API
output.

## In scope

- Required capability/compatibility manifest and fail-closed registry gate.
- Conformance, security, native-first, fallback, canonical serialization,
  configuration, paired LlamaParse latency where comparable, and independent
  resource entry gates.
- A test-only conforming future adapter and targeted nonconforming mutants.
- Reproducible CI/release evidence for every enabled adapter/version.

## Out of scope

- Shipping another production format adapter.
- Requiring formats to expose native capabilities they do not possess.
- Replacing Phase 08 privacy, license, canary, calibration, or operational
  release gates.

## Technical approach

Make registration/advertisement fail closed unless an adapter declares all
required policies and passes P07-US01 plus applicable security, native/fallback,
parity, canonical, and resource suites. Exercise every prohibited failure mode
with isolated mutants.

## Benchmark evidence — LlamaParse-15

- Current evidence: the PDF cases expose page-label, bbox, asset-origin,
  chart/diagram, and canonical-duplication risks, but contain no direct-image,
  scanned, or Office semantic twins.
- Generalized behavior: a future adapter cannot advertise a capability unless
  its manifest and applicable twin matrix demonstrate the same invariants with
  explicit format-specific tolerances.
- Acceptance/tests/metrics/performance: conforming positive adapter,
  capability-absent non-target, and missing-twin/origin/transform/duplicate/
  fabricated-structure/budget mutants; require deterministic gate results and
  paired LlamaParse p50/p95 where comparable, diagnostic local timings, and
  independently pinned RSS.
- Readiness: `GAP-COVERAGE-001` is fail-closed, not waivable by passing
  PDF-only snapshots.
- Gaps: `GAP-COVERAGE-001`, `GAP-BBOX-001`,
  `GAP-PROVENANCE-001`, `GAP-SERIALIZATION-001`, `GAP-PAGE-001`,
  and `GAP-PERFORMANCE-001`.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Every enabled adapter has a complete versioned capability/compatibility
   manifest and reproducible conformance result.
2. The sample future adapter passes shared IR, API/schema, canonical
   serialization, configuration, security, native/fallback, paired LlamaParse
   latency where comparable, and independent resource entry gates.
3. Mutants with missing provenance/transforms/limits, duplicate fallback,
   fabricated structure, native-evidence bypass, unsafe intake, or budget
   breach are rejected before enablement.
4. An unregistered or failing adapter cannot advertise a MIME/extension or
   activate through configuration.
5. Semantic-twin checks allow declared format-specific geometry tolerances
   without weakening text, type, relationship, concern, or grounding checks.
6. Gate results, manifest/adapter hashes, commands, versions, and environment
   are linked from completion evidence and reproduce deterministically.
7. Flag-off behavior retains the explicit allowlist of previously approved
   adapters.
8. An adapter with a missing applicable positive, non-target, or negative
   semantic-twin class is rejected before registration, and the gate reports
   coverage as missing rather than semantic parity as failed or zero.

## Dedicated tests

- Unit tests: capability/compatibility manifest, registry gates, capability
  negotiation, conforming sample adapter, and every nonconforming mutant.
- Integration tests:
  [tests/stories/phase_07/test_p07_us09_future_adapter_gate.py](../../../tests/stories/phase_07/test_p07_us09_future_adapter_gate.py)
- Negative tests: duplicate MIME, missing flag/provenance/transform/limit,
  unsafe intake/fallback, fabricated output, native bypass, duplicate merge,
  and budget breach.
- Contract tests: API/schema, unsupported-format behavior, canonical
  JSON/Markdown/text, and additive registration diagnostics.
- Fixture-based tests: conforming test adapter and isolated mutants for each
  prohibited failure mode.
- Coverage tests: missing direct-image/scanned/Office twins and format-specific
  label/origin/transform controls.
- Cross-format tests: semantic twins with declared geometry tolerance.
- Configuration, security, performance, and regression tests: disabled,
  unregistered, failing, and approved states across the Phase 07 matrix.

The sole operative latency benchmark is
[`latency-reference-v1.md`](../../benchmarks/llamaparse-15/latency-reference-v1.md).
A future adapter cannot claim a latency pass without semantically comparable
Llama input/output and, before Definition of Done, at least five interleaved
candidate/Llama samples per case. Candidate p50 and nearest-rank p95 must each
be no greater than paired Llama values, with no dropped failures,
corpus-average masking, or reduction in required quality/reliability. Otherwise
latency is `Unmeasured/Blocked`; local adapter timing cannot substitute. RSS,
security, compatibility, coverage, output, default-off, and rollback gates
remain independently blocking.

## Regression impact

Run Phase 00–07, every enabled adapter's dedicated/conformance suite, OOXML
security, native/fallback reconciliation, cross-format twins, canonical
serializers, API/schema, configuration, performance, and unchanged-format
fixtures.

## Metrics

- Before: future adapters have reusable tests but no mandatory enablement gate.
- Target: 100% enabled-adapter conformance; 0 unregistered/nonconforming
  adapters enabled; 100% prohibited mutants rejected; 100% approved
  semantic-twin parity; 100% advertised capabilities have applicable twin
  coverage; paired LlamaParse latency passed where comparable; RSS budgets met
  independently; local timing reported diagnostically.
- After: `../metrics.md`.

## API and schema compatibility

No production format is added. Registry/manifests remain internal or additive
diagnostics; unsupported-format responses and approved adapter output remain
compatible.

## Risks

- An overly rigid gate can suppress legitimate format-specific behavior.
- Test adapters cannot represent every future-format complexity.
- Capability/resource thresholds and paired-comparator configuration can drift
  without Phase 08 ownership.
- Teams may attempt to waive gates instead of narrowing unsupported scope.

## Rollback

Disable candidate future-adapter registration and retain the explicit allowlist
of already approved adapters. A failing adapter remains disabled; rollback does
not permit bypassing conformance.
