## User story

As a spreadsheet consumer, I want workbook, cell, formula, table, and visibility
semantics preserved as native evidence, so that values remain traceable to
their coordinates without executing spreadsheet logic.

## Business value

Avoids lossy worksheet screenshots, preserves typed data and formulas for
automation, and creates the native substrate for later Office-chart and visual
fallback stories.

## Problem statement

The parser has no XLSX adapter. Rendering first obscures sheet/cell identity,
formulas, merged/table ranges, and visibility, while calculating formulas or
mixing chart/fallback behavior into this story would be unsafe or oversized.

## In scope

- Workbook/sheet order and policy-visible cells, typed values, formulas, cached
  results, shared strings, dates/errors, merged ranges, and native tables.
- Sheet/row/column visibility, bounded used range, and cell/resource limits.
- Workbook/sheet/cell/part locators and table/relationship provenance.
- Chart/drawing placeholders and concerns for P07-US07/P07-US08.

## Out of scope

- Formula execution/recalculation or external workbook fetching.
- Native chart extraction, which belongs to P07-US07.
- Rendering or visual fallback, which belongs to P07-US08.
- Legacy `.xls`, macros, full print-layout reproduction, or unbounded blank
  styled cells.

## Technical approach

Traverse approved workbook, sheet, shared-string, style, table, and relationship
parts from P07-US03. Emit typed native cell/table evidence, retain formula and
cached value separately, enforce visibility/used-range policy, and defer
charts/drawings as linked placeholders.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Approved fixtures preserve workbook/sheet order, visible cell coordinates,
   typed values, formulas, cached values, merged ranges, and native tables
   exactly once.
2. Every emitted cell records `native_xml`, workbook/sheet/cell and part/XML
   locators; formulas are retained but never executed.
3. Formula and cached/display values remain distinct, with missing/stale or
   conflicting cache states explicit rather than silently resolved.
4. Hidden sheets/rows/columns follow documented policy with zero accidental
   visible-content leakage.
5. Sparse/oversized ranges honor configured sheet/cell/memory/time limits and
   fail with reason-coded concerns rather than expanding unboundedly.
6. Chart/drawing parts are linked placeholders with concerns; no chart values
   or visual fallback are produced in this story.
7. Unsafe packages/external relationships fail through P07-US03, and flag-off
   restores prior unsupported-format behavior.

## API and schema compatibility

Adds a flagged XLSX MIME/extension path and additive workbook/cell provenance.
Formula and cached value remain distinct; existing format responses are
unchanged and chart/fallback output is deferred.

## Risks

- Formula caches may be absent, stale, locale-dependent, or inconsistent.
- Excel date systems, errors, shared formulas, and sparse dimensions are easy
  to misinterpret.
- Large workbooks can exhaust CPU or memory without early bounds.
- OOXML reader dependencies require license/security review.

## Rollback

Disable the XLSX adapter and stop advertising XLSX support. Return the existing
unsupported-format response without formula execution or full-sheet rendering.
