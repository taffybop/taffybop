## User story

As a chart consumer, I want axes, panels, categories, legends, and series linked
to marks, so that visual structure is explicit before values are measured.

## Business value

Turns flat OCR into auditable chart structure and restores repeated years and the
`1H`/`Annual total` semantics.

## Problem statement

Current output has region labels and ticks as text, loses positional repetition,
and rejects the short legend token.

## In scope

- Linear-axis detection/calibration with residual.
- Panel, category-position, legend-swatch, series-color association.
- Units and unresolved/ambiguous structure.

## Out of scope

- Numeric mark values.
- Log/dual-axis implementation beyond explicit unsupported detection.

## Technical approach

Combine vector geometry with spatial OCR/glyph tokens, fit candidate linear axes,
and require consistent swatch/color/position evidence for series.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Four region panels, 11 category positions per panel, and visible year anchors are represented.
2. Axis ticks 25–125 fit one linear scale within approved residual.
3. Dark=`1H` and light=`Annual total` associate with evidence; ambiguity is flagged.
4. All repeated label occurrences keep bboxes.
5. Log/dual/ambiguous axes are detected as unsupported, not misfit linearly.

## Functional-fidelity follow-up — explicit PDF-layer chart organization

Validated 2026-08-13 for clean-energy, eGov, ESG, health, manufacturing, and
Uber:

- Exact repeated PDF-layer occurrences remain separate labels with authored
  page coordinates and `explicit_text` provenance; noisy OCR is never mixed
  into an exact promoted primary.
- Printed year/category labels and collinear numeric ticks may form bounded
  panels and unresolved x/y axes. Panel and tick evidence closes over the
  exact source token IDs. No scale is calibrated and no series/point/value is
  emitted unless the existing downstream proof contracts independently admit
  it.
- If categories, tick alignment, or source-layer completeness cannot be
  proven, the chart retains explicit labels plus
  `chart_source_text_structure_partial`; OCR-visible categories remain in the
  primary presentation rather than being dropped.
- Real-corpus checks confirm source-grounded axes/panels for eGov, ESG,
  health, manufacturing, and Uber while every affected output still has zero
  inferred series and zero inferred points.
