## User story

As a workflow-document consumer, I want explicit diagram nodes and connectors
grounded, so that clean flow relationships are machine-readable.

## Business value

Supports flowcharts and process diagrams without claiming semantic relations
that the current stack cannot prove.

## Problem statement

Predecessor diagram items exposed OCR and a
`diagram_relationships_not_structured` concern without topology.

## In scope

- Common node shapes, labels, connector paths/arrows, and explicit topology.
- Node/edge evidence, direction confidence, and ambiguity.
- Safe fallback for unsupported diagrams.

## Out of scope

- Domain-semantic interpretation.
- Implied, hidden, or unlabeled relationships.
- Model-assisted reasoning.

## Technical approach

Combine shape/line geometry with spatial labels, associate connector endpoints,
and require clear arrow/direction evidence.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Clean flowchart fixtures recover grounded nodes and directed edges.
2. Every node/edge links to source geometry and label evidence where present.
3. Crossed, disconnected, or ambiguous connectors remain unresolved.
4. No inferred semantic relationship appears without an explicit path.
5. Unsupported diagrams retain labels, bbox, confidence, provenance, and
   concerns.
