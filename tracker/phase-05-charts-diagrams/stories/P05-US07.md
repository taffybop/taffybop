## User story

As a raster-chart consumer, I want supported vertical bars measured against
grounded axes, so that approximate values remain traceable to source pixels.

## Business value

Extends the validated chart contract to common raster bars without presenting
pixel-derived estimates as literal or exact source data.

## Problem statement

P05-US06 provides raster axes, categories, and legends but deliberately emits no
marks or values. Vertical bar heights require separate pixel geometry,
group/stack association, and uncertainty handling.

## In scope

- Simple, grouped, and stacked vertical bars on supported 2-D linear axes.
- Bar/segment pixel geometry, baseline, category, and series association.
- `raster_measured` values with mark/axis evidence and pixel-derived tolerance.
- Unresolved output for clipped, occluded, or ambiguous bars.

## Out of scope

- Horizontal, log, dual-axis, 3-D, pictorial, waterfall, or floating bars.
- Raster line, scatter, area, or pie charts.
- Exact hidden source data or semantic correction of measured values.

## Technical approach

Detect supported vertical rectangular marks, associate them with P05-US06 axes,
categories, and legends, measure their baseline/height in pixel coordinates,
and pass candidate values and tolerances through P05-US05 validation.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Approved simple, grouped, and stacked vertical-bar fixtures recover all
   supported bar/segment marks with pixel bboxes.
2. Every emitted value references its mark, calibrated axis, category, series,
   method, confidence, and numeric tolerance.
3. Explicit printed labels remain `explicit_text`; height-derived values are
   `raster_measured`.
4. Values meet each fixture's approved pixel-derived tolerance.
5. Clipped, occluded, ambiguous, and unsupported bars emit no value and retain
   fallback structure plus concerns.
6. No line or non-bar mark is emitted by this story.

## API and schema compatibility

Adds default-off raster bar marks/values through the existing chart schema;
legacy and P05-US05 fallback output remain available.

## Risks

- Anti-aliasing and compression can blur bar boundaries.
- Grid lines or decorative rectangles can resemble bars.
- Pixel resolution limits the accuracy of low-height values.

## Rollback

Disable raster bar values; retain P05-US06 structure and P05-US05 fallback.

## Benchmark evidence — LlamaParse-15

- Gaps: `GAP-CHART-002`, `GAP-BBOX-001`,
  `GAP-PROVENANCE-001`, and `GAP-DIAGNOSTICS-001`.
- Source/current behavior: the 15-case corpus contains vector bar charts
  (catastrophe p1, clean-energy p1, Uber slide 2) and a raster line chart
  (manufacturing p3), but no approved source raster-bar positive. Vector cases
  must not be relabeled as raster evidence, and manufacturing p3 is a negative
  chart-type control.
- Generalized behavior: only reviewed 2-D linear vertical raster bars reach
  pixel measurement; every segment carries mark/baseline/axis/category/series
  evidence and tolerance, while unsupported or ambiguous bars stay fallback.
- Test slice: a reviewed synthetic/direct-image and PDF-render raster-bar
  positive is required before Ready; vector bars are non-targets; horizontal,
  log, 3-D, clipped, occluded, missing-baseline, and line-chart negatives.
- Acceptance/metrics/performance: 100% supported marks grounded and within
  tolerance, 100% method accuracy, and 0 unsupported values. Record pixel count,
  peak RSS, output size, and fallback rate; retain the local M0 2,589.5 MiB
  memory guard. The sole latency benchmark is
  [`LlamaParse latency reference v1`](../../benchmarks/llamaparse-15/latency-reference-v1.md).
  Initial one-shot values are planning only. Before Definition of Done, collect
  at least five interleaved candidate/Llama observations for every applicable
  case and require candidate p50 and inclusive nearest-rank p95 to be no
  greater than paired Llama p50/p95 per case, without average masking or
  dropped failures. Semantically incomparable input/output is
  **Unmeasured/Blocked**; retired local timings cannot substitute, and latency
  cannot compensate for a quality or reliability failure.
- Flag/rollback: `parser.charts.raster_bar_values.enabled`; disabling it removes
  raster bar values and retains P05-US06 structure/P05-US05 fallback.
