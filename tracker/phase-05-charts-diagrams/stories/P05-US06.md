## User story

As an image-chart consumer, I want visible labels, axes, and legends represented
spatially, so that later measurement starts from grounded raster structure.

## Business value

Makes raster charts useful before value measurement, preserves repeated labels,
and gives bar and line analyzers a common evidence-backed structure.

## Problem statement

Tesseract currently yields flat text. It does not assign titles, category
labels, ticks, units, or legend text and swatches to axes and series, and text
deduplication can erase spatial repetition.

## In scope

- Raster chart titles, axis lines, ticks, tick labels, units, categories, and
  legend labels/swatches.
- Spatial OCR evidence, repeated-label preservation, and structure confidence.
- Explicit unresolved structure and concerns when association is ambiguous.

## Out of scope

- Detecting or measuring bar, line, point, or other data marks.
- Emitting data-point values, including explicit printed-value association.
- Direct-image/PDF-render parity, resource-budget, or phase fallback gates.
- VLM use.

## Technical approach

Combine word-level OCR evidence with conventional axis-line, tick, and
legend-swatch geometry. Assign roles and relationships through the shared chart
schema while leaving every data series/value unresolved for later stories.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Approved clean raster fixtures recover all expected title, category, tick,
   unit, and legend labels with source bboxes.
2. Axis lines, ticks, and legend swatches link to the correct text evidence and
   chart region.
3. Repeated category labels at different coordinates remain distinct.
4. Ambiguous axis or legend associations remain unresolved with concerns.
5. The story emits no bar/line marks or data-point values on any fixture.
6. Flag-off output remains the P05-US05 chart fallback.

## Risks

- Axis and decorative-line false positives.
- OCR errors can misassign short tick or legend labels.
- Structure heuristics may overfit conventional chart layouts.

## Rollback
