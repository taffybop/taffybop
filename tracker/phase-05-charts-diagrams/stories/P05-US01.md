## User story

As a multimodal consumer, I want charts and diagrams represented consistently
even when structure is unavailable, so that unsupported content is useful but not fabricated.

## Business value

Creates a safe contract for deterministic and model-assisted analyzers and fixes
generic-image routing when optional classifiers are absent.

## Problem statement

The fixed baseline now identifies 14 chart items and 2 diagram items, but emits
zero structured chart series/values and zero structured diagram nodes/edges.
Type routing and fallback concerns are inconsistent across classifier,
classifier-unavailable, and ambiguous visual paths.

## In scope

- Chart/diagram element extensions, labels, axes, legends, series, points,
  nodes/connectors, methods, tolerances, concerns, and fallback.
- Deterministic visual-region heuristics when classifier artifacts are unavailable.

## Out of scope

- Extracting actual chart values or diagram edges.
- Visual-model calls.

## Technical approach

Add typed IR extensions and a conservative classifier-unavailable path based on
layout label, primitive density, axes/legend evidence, and unresolved fallback.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Catastrophe remains `chart` or `chart_candidate` when classifier artifacts
   are absent, and all reviewed chart/diagram regions use the same schema.
2. Unsupported charts retain caption, labels, bbox, evidence, and `values_not_structured`.
3. Diagrams have a parallel safe fallback.
4. No series/value/relationship field is populated without evidence.
5. Existing natural images remain images on negative fixtures.

## Functional-fidelity follow-up — classifier-unavailable source captions

Validated 2026-08-13 against hash-bound LlamaParse-15 PDFs and the absolute
offline Docling artifact profile:

- A raw graph-declared caption may route an otherwise unclassified content
  region only when picture/caption geometry is a unique same-page external
  match, accepted OCR has at least four lines and six words, and aggregate OCR
  confidence clears the configured classification floor.
- Diagram captions require an explicit diagram/flowchart term. Chart captions
  require an explicit chart-domain term plus at least three source OCR numeric
  tokens. No chart value, series, node, connector, or relationship is inferred
  from the caption.
- Photograph, board, generic image, logo/icon/screenshot/illustration, sparse
  OCR, low-confidence OCR, table-like, overlapping, distant, and ambiguous
  caption fixtures remain images.
- A caption-routed chart/diagram whose predecessor already approved owner OCR
  for primary output must retain that OCR exactly once in JSON, Markdown, and
  rendered UI. The external caption remains separately owned and occurs once;
  it is never folded into owner OCR.
- Region provenance retains the public item ID, raw picture source ID, raw
  caption source ID(s), and caption SHA-256.

## Functional-fidelity follow-up — native visual text and uncaptained routing

Validated 2026-08-13 against the hash-bound source PDFs and retained
LlamaParse/UI artifacts:

- A routed PDF chart or diagram may retain exact authored text from the native
  PDF layer only when every occurrence and reconstructed line is bounded,
  finite, uniquely identified, same-unit, and physically contained by the
  visual owner. The text, occurrence count, line closure, and SHA-256 must
  agree exactly through the P05 and P03 projections.
- Native visual text replaces primary OCR only for an already routed `chart`
  or `diagram`. Arbitrary images, forged hashes, missing/duplicate lineage,
  out-of-owner boxes, fused fiscal-year/footnote glyphs, and a native layer
  missing OCR-visible year categories remain diagnostic/subordinate.
- An uncaptained image may route as a chart only from an explicit printed
  signature: bounded currency/year callouts, energy percentages/labels,
  fiscal rows with numeric cells, or dense year/numeric chart-domain labels.
  Photos, boards, forms, tables, decorative regions, and sparse prose remain
  non-targets.
- Exact source labels correct eGov OCR (`AO` to `40`, `4A` to `44`) in JSON,
  Markdown, canonical Markdown, and rendered DOM. Health's character-split
  native glyph stream is reconstructed as authored lines. ESG's two
  uncaptained regions, manufacturing's first uncaptained page-1 region, and
  Uber's two page-2 regions are typed as charts without emitting inferred
  series or points.
- OCR and all raw diagnostics remain serialized. No Llama-generated chart
  value, interpolated mark value, or semantic prose is copied into service
  output.
- Promoting exact native visual text may replace `value`, `md`, and `source`,
  but `detected_text` remains a strict boolean detection flag. The authored
  text stays in `visual_source_text`; a string-valued `detected_text` is a
  public JSON contract failure.
