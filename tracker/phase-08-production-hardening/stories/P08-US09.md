## User story

As a data-governance owner, I want hosted parsing routes blocked unless their
privacy and location policy is approved, so that document regions cannot leave
the local boundary accidentally.

## Business value

Fail-closed governance makes optional hosted quality available only where
tenant, data, vendor, retention, and residency obligations permit it.

## Problem statement

Hosted adapters can be technically default-off yet still lack one enforceable
release decision covering data classification, consent, crop scope, redaction,
retention, residency, provider/model version, and egress destination.

## In scope

- Data-classification and tenant-policy inputs.
- Provider/model/region allowlists and artifact-version linkage.
- Region-only egress, redaction eligibility, retention/cache, residency, and
  subprocessor approval checks.
- Deny reason codes, approval expiry, and auditable test-double decisions.
- Local fallback when hosted policy denies a request or release.

## Out of scope

- Granting legal, privacy, procurement, or tenant approval.
- Choosing a hosted provider.
- Sending real document content during tests.

## Technical approach

Evaluate a versioned policy before a hosted capability can be released or
called. Bind approvals to the P08-US08 manifest, tenant/data class, provider,
model, region, retention, and crop scope. Treat missing or expired evidence as
denial and route to local fallback.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Hosted processing is denied unless tenant permission, data class, provider,
   model version, region, retention/cache, residency, and subprocessor status
   are all approved.
2. Approval is bound to a manifest version and expires or denies when any bound
   provider/model/policy value changes.
3. Only approved regions and fields are included in the test-double egress
   request; whole-document escalation is rejected unless explicitly permitted.
4. Missing, expired, contradictory, or malformed policy fails closed with an
   actionable reason and invokes the documented local fallback.
5. Policy logs and telemetry contain decisions and bounded reason codes but no
   source content, prompts, secrets, or response payloads.
6. No test performs live external egress.

## API and schema compatibility

No public schema change. A denied hosted route preserves the documented local
fallback and existing error/concern behavior.

## Risks

- Policy inputs can drift from contractual reality.
- Overbroad approvals can expose more content than necessary.
- Provider retention or subprocessor terms may change independently.

## Rollback

Deny all hosted routes and use local P06 fallback. Restoring a route requires a
new manifest-bound approval, never a bypass.
