  calibrated probabilities.
- Sequence: after basic P08-US03/US04 signals and completed deterministic paths;
  feeds P08-US07.

Release completion is conservative confidence behavior, not statistical

## User story

As a downstream consumer, I want text, layout, and table confidence calibrated
on held-out evidence, so that deterministic outputs are accepted or withheld
according to measured risk rather than unrelated engine scores.

## Business value

Reliable deterministic confidence reduces silent corruption and unnecessary
review without adding model cost to ordinary documents.

## Problem statement

Native, OCR, detection, relationship, and table scores have different meanings.
They are not calibrated consistently against source truth by content type.

## In scope

- Versioned fit/evaluation partitions for text, layout, and tables.
- Separate text, detection, structure, and completeness calibration.
- Per-type accept, concern, and conservative-fallback thresholds.
- Reliability, ECE, Brier, coverage, and error-by-category reports.

## Out of scope

- Chart, diagram, or model-generated confidence.
- Review packet construction or queue routing.
- Replacing source evidence with calibrated probability.

## Technical approach

Use the P00 metric contract and frozen truth to fit approved simple calibration
methods on training partitions and evaluate disjoint holdouts. Keep dimensions
separate and abstain when a category lacks sufficient evidence.

## Benchmark evidence — LlamaParse-15

- Cases/regions: `ny-timetable` tables have null confidence despite 12/13-column
  structure and missing rows; `postal-10k` table confidence is null at the
  detached last row; purchase/settlement items are also unscored despite order,
  formatting, and lexical errors. Expert near-perfect page scores likewise miss
  concrete errors.
- Current behavior: page/OCR/bbox scores do not express text correctness,
  table structure/completeness, reading order, or page-label fidelity.
- Generalized behavior: calibrate dimensions separately against source truth
  and force conservative fallback where a type/category lacks sufficient
  evidence.
- Acceptance/tests/metrics/performance: verified tables/text positives,
  unchanged raw-score non-targets, and grid/boundary/order/hyphen hard
  negatives; report category ECE/Brier/coverage and diagnostic lookup timing.
- Gaps: `GAP-BBOX-001`, `GAP-PROVENANCE-001`,
  `GAP-DIAGNOSTICS-001`, `GAP-SERIALIZATION-001`, and `GAP-PAGE-001`.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Text, layout, and table datasets have versioned, leakage-checked, disjoint
   fit/evaluation partitions.
2. Each supported confidence dimension has an approved target and held-out ECE,
   Brier, coverage, and error report by document category.
3. A category is enabled only when it meets its approved targets; otherwise it
   remains raw and uses the documented conservative fallback.
4. OCR confidence is never serialized as table-structure, layout-completeness,
   or source-correctness confidence.
5. Corrupt-font, caption, reading-order, merged-cell, and negative-control
   fixtures produce the expected accept/concern/fallback decision.
6. Disabling the flag restores raw confidence and P07 routing exactly.
7. Held-out controls include the NY 13-to-12 grid collapse, Postal detached
   `FERS`, purchase top-matter order/redline loss, settlement lexical hyphen,
   and clean-table positives; no unrelated page/OCR/bbox score may promote the
   failing dimension.

## API and schema compatibility

Calibrated values are additive and dimension-labeled. Existing raw confidence
remains available and does not silently change meaning.

## Risks

- Sparse categories can appear calibrated by chance.
- One threshold can hide format-specific failures.
- Fixture drift can invalidate calibration without code changes.

## Rollback

Disable `parser.confidence.deterministic_calibration.enabled`; retain raw
confidence, provenance, validators, and conservative fallback.
