## User story

As an on-call and release owner, I want a production-like failure-injection and
rollback drill, so that containment and known-good recovery are proven before
release.

## Business value

The drill verifies that technical controls and human procedures restore service
within the approved objective under representative failures.

## Problem statement

Flags, telemetry, gates, and runbooks are planning claims until controlled
failures exercise capability rollback and the complete known-good restoration
path.

## In scope

- Production-like candidate deployment using synthetic or approved fixtures.
- Controlled quality, paired LlamaParse latency, memory, artifact, privacy,
  exporter, review, and external-route failures.
- Capability-specific containment and full-release rollback.
- Recovery-time, compatibility, fixture, artifact, configuration, and evidence
  verification.
- Phase completion and release-blocking report.

## Out of scope

- Live production traffic or customer documents.
- Destructive infrastructure failure testing.
- Waiving a failed recovery check.

## Technical approach

Execute P08-US11 steps against the pinned candidate and known-good releases,
inject bounded failures, and validate each stop/rollback result through health,
contract, fixture, telemetry, and manifest checks. Preserve all evidence.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Every approved injected scenario is detected and triggers its documented
   stop, fallback, capability rollback, or full rollback.
2. Capability rollback disables one optional model, hosted route, or format
   without disabling unaffected local-core parsing.
3. Full rollback restores known-good artifacts, flags, calibration, policy,
   API/schema, errors, JSON/Markdown/text, and fixture results.
4. Recovery completes within the approved objective and records timing for
   detection, decision, containment, restoration, and verification.
5. Exporter/review-route failure does not prevent parsing or rollback evidence
   collection through the approved fallback channel.
6. The phase report records 100% required evidence, 0 known-good fixture
   differences, 0 content leaks, and every unresolved failure as release
   blocking.

- Performance tests: detection, containment, and full recovery timing.

recovery-time objectives are operational safety gates, not substitute latency
benchmarks, and remain independently blocking with memory/resource, quality,
security/privacy, compatibility, custody/hosted-use, default-off, and rollback.

## Risks

- The reference environment may omit production failure modes.
- Injection can exceed intended bounds if controls are weak.
- Rollback fails when known-good artifacts or configuration are mutable.

## Rollback

If known-good recovery fails, stop release activity, keep optional routes
disabled, preserve evidence, and treat the result as a blocking incident.
