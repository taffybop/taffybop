## User story

As a quality and cost owner, I want grounded outcome and route telemetry, so
that fallback behavior, unsupported content, escalation, and external spend are
measurable without treating proxies as accuracy.

## Business value

Outcome visibility detects quality drift and uncontrolled escalation while
keeping document contents out of operational systems.

## Problem statement

Concern, provenance, fallback, validator, model-route, and cost evidence exists
in different structures and is not reconciled into bounded operational signals.

## In scope

- Concern, omission/duplication, provenance-coverage, validator, unsupported,
  fallback, and abstention counts.
- Local/model/hosted route, retry, timeout, escalation, and review-request
  signals.
- Attributable request/token/credit/currency-neutral cost units from approved
  provider records or test doubles.
- Reconciliation against fixture truth and provider-call records.

## Out of scope

- Claiming live extraction accuracy without ground truth.
- Recording source values, prompts, responses, or review content.
- Confidence calibration or review routing.

## Technical approach

Emit bounded events at finalized validation and routing decisions, classify
them by controlled content/route enums, and derive aggregate quality proxies
without exposing the underlying claim. Reconcile external cost only from
versioned provider usage records.

## Benchmark evidence — LlamaParse-15

- Cases/regions: `ny-timetable` has a critical 12-column table with no table
  concern; `postal-10k` labels false `ClO` and incomplete `FERS` as recovery;
  `uber-earnings` correctly signals unstructured charts but misses false
  caption/hidden-text/duplicate failures, and three OCR orientation failures
  appear only on stderr while structured document warnings remain empty.
- Current behavior: concern counts and token-recall proxies do not distinguish
  correct diagnostics, silent failures, false positives, or source/generated/
  derived origin.
- Generalized behavior: reconcile finalized quality/fallback/escalation events
  against fixture truth by bounded gap/content classes, without treating
  self-confidence or expert agreement as accuracy.
- Acceptance/tests/metrics/performance: positive true concerns, non-target
  clean fixtures, and silent/false/duplicate diagnostic negatives; reconcile
  route/cost once and measure telemetry timing for diagnosis.
- Gaps: `GAP-VISUAL-001`, `GAP-CHART-001`, `GAP-CHART-002`,
  `GAP-DIAGRAM-001`, `GAP-PROVENANCE-001`,
  `GAP-DIAGNOSTICS-001`, `GAP-SERIALIZATION-001`, and
  `GAP-PAGE-001`.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Approved fixtures reconcile 100% of concerns, fallbacks, abstentions,
   validator failures, and escalation decisions to emitted signals.
2. Provenance coverage is computed from evidence relationships and never from
   parser self-confidence.
3. Every local/model/hosted call, retry, timeout, and fallback is counted once.
4. Test-double usage records reconcile 100% with attributable cost signals;
   unknown cost is explicit rather than estimated silently.
5. No document text, filename, crop, prompt, model response, or review content
   appears in labels or structured events.
6. Disabling the flag preserves output and makes no quality/cost telemetry
   calls.
7. Approved benchmark fixtures distinguish correctly detected, missed, and
   false-positive diagnostics and reconcile explicit, derived, generated, and
   unverifiable origin counts; token similarity or confidence alone cannot
   satisfy the assertion.
8. Page-label mismatch, duplicate canonical content, and generated-as-source
   events are counted once using bounded reason enums and no source content.

## API and schema compatibility

No required public change. Existing evidence and concerns remain authoritative;
telemetry does not redefine their semantics.

## Risks

- Operational proxies can be misreported as true quality.
- Provider usage records may arrive late or omit cost.
- Multiple fallback layers can double count one decision.

## Rollback

Disable `parser.telemetry.quality.enabled`; keep parser validation, fallbacks,
and P08-US02 telemetry infrastructure unchanged.
