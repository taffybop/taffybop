# P02-US05 — Make OCR token cleanup numeric-safe

Status: Done  
Story points: 2  
Phase: Phase 2 — Text Integrity  
Priority: High  
Dependencies: P00-US03  
Feature flag: `parser.ocr.numeric_cleanup_v2.enabled`

## User story

As a chart/table consumer, I want numeric tokens preserved unless identifier
context proves they are split hexadecimal text, so that years are not concatenated.

## Business value

Removes a deterministic corruption with small code/test scope and no model cost.

## Problem statement

`[A-F0-9]{2,}` treats pure years as hex and joins twelve labels into a 48-digit string.

## In scope

- Contextual hex-join eligibility.
- Pure-decimal and year safeguards.
- Genuine split-hash compatibility.

## Out of scope

- Spatial deduplication.
- Chart structure or OCR recognition.

## Technical approach

Require a non-decimal hex signal and identifier context/pattern before joining;
leave pure digit runs tokenized.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Twelve chart years remain twelve tokens.
2. No 48-digit joined year string is emitted.
3. Approved genuine long split-hex fixtures still normalize correctly.
4. Dates, monetary values, page numbers, and ordinary numeric lists remain unchanged.
5. Flag-off behavior reproduces the baseline.

## Risks

## Rollback
